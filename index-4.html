Our story

Built Different.

I was a teenager with a vision and no blueprint. Nobody handed me the rules of how a clothing brand is supposed to start.

So I figured it out myself, step by step: the ideas, the designs, the brand, even this website. Built from scratch, one late night at a time.

It all comes back to one idea: control your drip. DRIPCTRL® is just getting started.

FAQ

What sizes are available?

Sizes S, M, L and XL are listed. Both tees are oversized, so your usual size gives the intended loose fit. See the size chart in Support or on any product page.

How do I order?

Open a piece, choose a size and quantity, add it to your cart and check out.

How do I track my order?

Use Track order in the Support section below and enter your order ID.

What about shipping and returns?

We ship pan-India. See Shipping and Returns in the Support section below for timelines and policies.

Are the photos the real garments?

Yes. The images show the actual designs.

What currency are prices in?

All prices are in Indian rupees (₹).
